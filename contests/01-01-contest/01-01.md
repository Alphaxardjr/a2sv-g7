## Universal Ceil Method

The **Universal Ceil Method** is a handy technique often used in competitive programming.  

### Applications
- Grouping items  
- Packing problems  
- Finding the number of containers needed  
- Batching  

### Formula

(n + d - 1) // d



This formula answers the question:  
**How many groups of size `d` do I need for `n` items?**
